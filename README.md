# ❄️ Study of Heat Transfer Characteristics in a Peltier Thermoelectric Cooling System

### Thermoelectric Cooling | Heat Transfer Analysis | Experimental Investigation

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on the experimental investigation of heat transfer characteristics in a Peltier-based thermoelectric cooling system.

A thermoelectric cooling system utilizes the Peltier effect to produce a temperature difference between two surfaces when DC electrical power is supplied. One side absorbs heat (cold side), while the other side releases heat (hot side).

The project investigates the cooling performance, temperature variation, heat dissipation, and coefficient of performance (COP) of a thermoelectric cooling system under different operating voltage conditions.

A TEC1-12706 Peltier module is used along with aluminium heat sinks, cooling fans, and temperature sensors to develop and evaluate the cooling arrangement.

The experimental investigation is carried out at different input voltages to understand how electrical operating conditions influence the thermal performance of the system.

## 🎯 Objectives

- To study the working principle of thermoelectric cooling using the Peltier effect.
- To investigate heat transfer between the hot and cold sides of the Peltier module.
- To analyze temperature variations under different input voltages.
- To evaluate the cooling performance of the TEC1-12706 module.
- To study the influence of heat sinks and forced-air cooling on heat dissipation.
- To determine and compare the coefficient of performance (COP).
- To identify the relationship between electrical input and thermal performance.

## 🔬 Working Principle

The system operates based on the **Peltier Effect**.

When direct current passes through two dissimilar semiconductor materials connected electrically in series and thermally in parallel, heat is absorbed at one junction and released at the other.

The system produces:

- **Cold Side:** Absorbs heat from the surrounding region.
- **Hot Side:** Rejects absorbed heat along with the electrical energy supplied to the module.
- **Heat Sink:** Transfers heat away from the hot side.
- **Cooling Fan:** Enhances convective heat transfer and improves heat dissipation.

The direction of current determines which side becomes hot and which becomes cold.

## 📐 Important Calculations

### 1. Electrical Input Power

P = V × I

Where:
- P = Electrical input power (W)
- V = Applied voltage (V)
- I = Input current (A)

### 2. Temperature Difference

ΔT = Th - Tc

Where:
- Th = Hot-side temperature
- Tc = Cold-side temperature

### 3. Coefficient of Performance (COP)

COP = Qc / P

Where:
- Qc = Cooling capacity (W)
- P = Electrical input power (W)

COP represents the cooling effect produced per unit of electrical energy supplied.

### 4. Heat Transfer Analysis

The cooling and heat rejection performance can be studied using:

Qh = Qc + P

Where:
- Qh = Heat rejected at the hot side
- Qc = Heat absorbed at the cold side
- P = Electrical input power

This represents the ideal steady-state energy balance, neglecting other losses.
